VOLTOSHARE LOGO EXPORTS

All PNGs have transparent backgrounds. SVGs contain native vector paths.

Primary: forest-green symbol and Share, ink Volto and tagline. Use on light.
Navigation: symbol and wordmark only, with the tagline intentionally omitted.
White: complete all-white lockup for dark backgrounds.
Black: complete monochrome black lockup.
Symbol: standalone green, white and black variants.

Recommended digital minimum widths: 320 px primary, 200 px navigation,
24 px symbol (32 px preferred). Keep clear space of at least one quarter
of the symbol height. Preserve aspect ratio and never retype the wordmark.

These are cleaned vector reconstructions from the supplied raster references.
The wordmark and tagline were traced from the high-resolution supplied dark
lockup. The symbol was traced from the supplied isolated black master.
They preserve the supplied appearance closely but are not original source
vectors or mathematically identical originals. No guessed font was used to
retype the logo. Original references are included in 07_References.

PNG exports: full lockups 3000 x 676; navigation 3000 x 516;
standalone symbols 1800 x 1727. Use SVG for scalable digital layout and
PNG for broad presentation/document compatibility.
